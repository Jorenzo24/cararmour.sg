A liquid applied over the factory clear coat that cures into a hard, chemically bonded layer. It is not a wax sitting on the surface: it becomes part of the paint system. The result is a slicker, more hydrophobic finish that resists etching from rain, bird droppings and UV, and washes far more easily. Ours carries a three-year coat bonding warranty.

Graphene is the newer chemistry. The Artdeshine Nano Graphene V3 we install is harder and more flexible than standard ceramic, so it moves with the panel instead of micro-cracking. It also retains less heat, which is noticeable on black and dark metallic cars parked in the open, and it disperses water faster. It carries a five-year warranty against three for ceramic.

Three years for ceramic, five for graphene, both backed in writing. Singapore is harder on paint than most places, with constant UV, heavy rain and high humidity, so what really decides the lifespan is washing. A coated car washed with pH-neutral shampoo and topped up at a maintenance session every six months will see out its warranty comfortably. Fortnightly trips through an automated brush wash will not.

They solve different problems. A coating is a few microns thick and changes how the surface behaves chemically, so you get gloss, water that sheets off and far less etching. Paint protection film is measured in tenths of a millimetre and absorbs stone chips. If your car lives on the expressway and chips worry you, you want film, usually on the front end only. Plenty of people end up with film on the nose and coating over everything.

A wax sits on top of the clear coat and Singapore rain takes it off in weeks. A coating bonds chemically and stays for years, which is why it comes with a written warranty and a wax never does. Beyond two years of ownership the coating is cheaper per month, and the paint ends up in better condition when you sell.

Almost always, and it is already in the price. A coating locks in whatever is underneath it, so applying one over swirl marks and water spots preserves them for three years. We clay, chemical-wash and machine-polish first, then bond the coating. On a brand new car the correction step is lighter, but the decontamination still happens.

Interior detailing takes about an hour. Coat maintenance takes an hour and a half, paint correction around two hours. Ceramic coating needs four to five hours and graphene five to six, because each layer has to flash off before the next goes on. We give you a finish time when we confirm the slot.
