07 FAQ

Questions we get
every week

If yours is not here, message us on WhatsApp. You will get an answer from someone who does the work, not a chatbot.

Interior detailing starts at S$100 and takes about an hour. Paint correction starts at S$238. A three-year ceramic coating runs from S$388 for a small or mid-size sedan to S$628 for a luxury or exotic car, and a five-year graphene coating from S$688. Every quote is all-in — claying, chemical wash and machine polishing are included rather than charged as prep.

Yes, anywhere in Singapore. We carry our own water, power and inspection lighting, so nothing depends on your building. A covered or sheltered lot is preferable for coating work, because the surface has to stay dry through the cure. If your spot will not work, we tell you before you book.

Interior detailing takes about an hour. Coat maintenance takes an hour and a half, paint correction around two hours. Ceramic coating needs four to five hours and graphene five to six, because each layer has to flash off before the next goes on. We give you a finish time when we confirm the slot.

A wax sits on top of the clear coat and is gone in weeks under Singapore rain. A coating bonds chemically and stays for years, which is why it comes with a written bonding warranty and a wax does not. If you keep a car more than two years, the coating is cheaper per month and the paint ends up in better condition.
