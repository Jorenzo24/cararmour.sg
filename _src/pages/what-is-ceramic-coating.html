01 The chemistry

A layer thinner than a hair, bonded to the clear coat

A ceramic coating is a liquid resin built mostly on silicon dioxide, the compound glass and quartz are made of. It is applied by hand, one panel at a time. Once its solvent evaporates, the resin reacts with moisture in the air and cross-links into a transparent film that is chemically bonded to the clear coat, the top layer of your factory paint.

The film is very thin. One layer is around a micron, while the clear coat beneath it usually measures 35 to 55 microns and a human hair about 70. You cannot see or feel the added thickness. What changes is the surface: smoother and harder than bare clear coat, and far less inclined to hold on to water and dirt.

In practice, rain beads up and runs off instead of drying into spots. Bird droppings, tree sap and road film sit on the coating rather than keying into the paint, so most of them come off with a normal wash.

The clear coat is the layer every polish removes a little of, and it cannot grow back. A coating takes the daily wear in its place, and it can be topped up or reapplied when it thins.

What a ceramic coating will not do

It will not stop stone chips or deep scratches: at around a micron, the layer is far too thin to absorb an impact. For chips, the answer is paint protection film, roughly 150 to 200 microns thick. The 9H rating printed on many bottles comes from a pencil hardness test run on a laboratory panel, and says nothing about a car parked in a busy carpark. A coating will not hide swirl marks either. It preserves whatever lies underneath, which is why the paint is machine-polished before it goes on.

06 Aftercare

Looking after a coated car

The coating does most of the work. A few habits decide how long it keeps performing.

  1. First 24 hours

    Keep it dry

    The coating is still curing. Rain on a fresh coating can leave marks in the surface.

  2. First week

    Water only

    Rinse without shampoo. Most coatings take about a week to reach full hardness.

  3. Every wash

    Gentle washing

    pH-neutral shampoo, two buckets, a clean microfibre to dry. Automated brush washes are best avoided.

  4. Every three months

    Coat maintenance

    We wash, clay, top up the detailer layer and treat the rims. Coat maintenance.

07 Questions

Ceramic coating questions

It resists the fine marks left by washing better than bare clear coat, but it will not stop a key, a trolley or a stone chip. A layer around one micron thick cannot absorb an impact of that kind. Paint protection film, a polyurethane layer of roughly 150 to 200 microns, is the product designed for chips.

9H is a grade on the pencil hardness test (ASTM D3363). Pencils of increasing hardness are pushed across a coated test panel, and the rating is the hardest lead that does not cut through the film. It describes the film on that panel in laboratory conditions. It is not a measure of how well a car resists scratches on the road, and it is not the Mohs mineral scale.

A new car is the best moment to coat. The paint needs very little polishing, so the coating goes over an almost perfect finish and keeps it that way. New cars still arrive with fallout from transport and storage, which is why the decontamination step happens on every car.
