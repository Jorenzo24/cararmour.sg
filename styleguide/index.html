08 Process

Déroulé numéroté

Compteur CSS en chiffres dorés, filets 1 px pleine largeur. Sert aux pages service et au bloc « how it works » de l'accueil.

  1. Tell us the car and the postcode

    Send the model and where it is parked over WhatsApp. We confirm the size band, the package and a two-hour arrival window.

  2. We come to you

    The team arrives with water, power and lighting. Covered carpark, condo lot or landed driveway — anywhere on the island.

  3. Correction, then coating

    Claying and chemical wash, machine polish to pull out swirl marks and water spots, then the coating is bonded layer by layer.

  4. Cure, inspect, hand back

    We walk you around the car under inspection light and leave you the aftercare sheet and your warranty reference.
